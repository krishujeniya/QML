A sensible route through the field

Your learning path

Move from prerequisites to careful experiments, one layer at a time.

Get the basics

Linear algebra, probability, and classical ML.

Learn quantum circuits

States, gates, measurement, and noise.

Study a model

Try a variational circuit or quantum kernel.

Test the claim

Use a classical baseline and report resource costs.

Keep the evidence in view

Quantum machine learning is an active research area. A quantum formulation alone does not establish a practical advantage; results depend on the task, data access, hardware, and comparison method.
